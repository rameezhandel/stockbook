Privacy Policy

Stockbook · Last updated 19 August 2026

Stockbook collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

Why this is verifiable, not just a promise

The Android app requests no permissions at all — including no internet permission. Android does not merely discourage network access without it; the system refuses to open a connection. The app therefore cannot transmit your data even in principle, whether by design, by accident, or through a future mistake.

There is no analytics library, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party software development kit of any kind in the app.

What the app stores, and where

All of it is written to your phone's private storage for this app, which no other app can read:

  • Your shop's name and address, and the products, prices and stock counts you enter.
  • Bills, deliveries, customers, suppliers, payments and credit notes.
  • Photographs you take of paper bills. These are stored inside the app only — they are not added to your photo gallery and are not visible to other apps.
  • Your choice of language, currency and theme.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. There is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

The camera and your photo library

If you choose to attach a photograph to a bill, the app either opens your phone's own camera app or your system photo picker. It receives only the single picture you select or take. It has no access to your photo library, and does not ask for any.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you deliberately send it, and only where you send it:

  • Exporting a backup file. You choose the location using your phone's own file picker. The app hands the file to the system and takes no further part.
  • Sharing a statement, a bill or a photograph. This opens your phone's standard share sheet. What happens next belongs to the app you pick — a messaging app, email, or your own storage — and to that app's own privacy policy.

The app never initiates any of this on its own, and never shares anything in the background.

Data we receive

None. We do not receive, see, store or process any of your information. We could not identify a single user of this app.

Your rights over your data

Because your data never leaves your phone, you already hold all of it. You can edit or delete any record inside the app, export everything to a file you keep, or remove all of it at once by uninstalling the app — which deletes the shop records and every stored photograph along with it.

There is no account to close and no deletion request to make, because there is nothing held anywhere else.

Children

Stockbook is a tool for running a business and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed with it. Any change that introduced data collection would be described here plainly and in advance.
